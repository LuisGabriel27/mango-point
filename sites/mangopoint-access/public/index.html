Before you send

A few useful answers.

Can I create an account immediately?

Accounts are provisioned for approved organizations after access arrangements are agreed. This website collects inquiries; it does not automatically grant access.

Where can I find pricing?

Use the request form to discuss your organization’s scope and licensing or subscription terms. Pricing is agreed during that conversation.

Can we request a demonstration first?

Yes. Choose “A system demonstration” in the form and describe your team and intended use.

We already have a MangoPoint account. Where do we sign in?

Use Customer sign in to open the existing MangoPoint application.
